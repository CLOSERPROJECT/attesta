Process Overview

Tamper-evident by design

01

Create Stream

Organization templates batch parameters or inspection guidelines via schema-gated API or clean form views.

02

Track Progress

Cerbos policies validate role capabilities and step-order as batches shift across physical processing handlers.

03

Verify & DPP

Record hash state is written to the cryptographic registry, automatically producing a GS1 Digital Product Passport Link.
